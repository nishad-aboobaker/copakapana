# Kitchen Station Screen Guide — Copakapana Restaurant

Each kitchen station (Fried Chicken, Juice Counter, Main Kitchen) has a screen that shows its orders (tickets).

## 1. Starting the screen
1. On the station tablet/monitor, open the app at `/login/kitchen` (or the installed app icon).
2. Tap your station, enter the station PIN and tap **→**.
3. Tap anywhere on the **"Tap to start"** screen. This turns on the sound and keeps the screen awake.
> The screen stays signed in all day. Keep it plugged in and on the restaurant WiFi.

## 2. Reading a ticket card
- **#014**: the ticket number. **Order #231**: the order number at the bottom.
- **First · T7** = dine-in table; **TAKEAWAY · name**; **DELIVERY · name** (with phone).
- The waiter's name and a **⏱ timer** since the ticket was sent. The card border turns **amber** when it's getting late and **red** when it's late.
- **Red "EDITED" banner**: a change to an earlier ticket (shown as "vs #014").
  - **ADD**: make these extra items.
  - **CANCEL** (struck through): stop or don't make these.
- **RESENT**: the cashier sent this ticket again.
- New and edited tickets chime and flash the screen.

## 3. Working the orders
Cards move left to right through three lanes:

| Lane | Button | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New** | **START ▶** | You've started cooking it |
| **Preparing** | **READY ✓** | Food is ready at the pass, and the waiter's phone is alerted |
| **Ready** | **CLEAR** | Food has been picked up (the waiter can also mark it served) |

- **↩** moves a card back one lane if you tapped too early.
- **↺ Recall**: brings back a card you cleared in the last 5 minutes.
- 🔔 / 🔕 mutes or unmutes the chime on this screen.

## 4. Running out of something
1. Tap **Stock** at the top of the screen.
2. Find the dish and tap **Out**. Waiters immediately see it greyed out as "Out of stock" and can't order it. If it was in someone's cart, it's removed with a warning.
3. When it's back, tap **In stock**.
- The **Stock** button shows a red count of dishes currently out.
- Each screen only lists its own station's dishes. Changes are logged with the station and the time.

## 5. If something goes wrong
- **"OFFLINE" / red bar at the top**: the screen lost its connection. It reconnects by itself and then shows every ticket you missed. The cashier can see that the screen is offline.
- **Screen went dark or the browser closed**: open it again and sign in. All open tickets come back.
- **A ticket looks wrong**: ask the waiter or cashier. Don't clear it until it's sorted.
