# Cashier Guide — Copakapana Restaurant

## 1. Signing in
Open `/login/pc` on the counter PC and sign in with your username and password. You land on the **Billing desk**. The menu on the left also has **Take orders**, if you need to enter an order yourself.

## 2. The billing desk
- **Top strip**: each kitchen screen and the bill printer shown green (online) or red (offline).
- **Left**: all open orders. Orders waiting for the bill are listed first and tagged **Bill asked** or **Billed**. When a waiter has already printed the bill from their phone, the order shows **Billed**: select it and take the payment as usual. Search by order number, table (e.g. `T5`) or customer name/phone, or filter by Dine-in / Takeaway / Delivery.
- **Middle**: the selected order's items and totals.
- **Right**: payment.

Selecting an order only *shows* it. Nothing changes until you press **Generate & print bill**.

## 3. Closing a bill
1. Select the order and press **Generate & print bill**. The bill prints on the counter printer straight away, the items are locked and the table is marked as billing. The bill is **not** settled yet.
2. Optional: **Add discount**. Choose percent or amount, pick or type a reason (required). The new total is previewed before you apply. Then press **Print bill again** so the customer gets the bill with the discount.
3. Take payment:
   - **Cash**: enter the cash received, or tap a quick amount (*Exact*, ₹500, …). The change to return is shown in green.
   - **UPI**: confirm the payment on your UPI app, then settle.
   - **Split**: enter the cash part; the rest is put on UPI automatically.
4. Press **Settle & save**. This saves the payment and frees the table. Nothing prints. A card shows the bill number, the amount and any change. If the customer wants a receipt with the payment on it, press **Reprint**.

Waiters can also print the bill from their phone (**Print bill**). That order then shows **Billed** here. Take the payment and press **Settle & save** as usual.

**Delivery orders:** **Generate & print bill** prints the delivery bill. Send it with the rider (need another copy? press **Print delivery bill again**). It shows the customer's name, phone and address in large print, the items, and **COLLECT ₹…** (the amount to take from the customer). When the rider comes back with the money, settle the bill as usual. If the customer already paid by UPI, settle first, then press **Print delivery bill** on the paid bill: the slip says **PAID**, so the rider collects nothing.

The order list shows where each delivery is: **On the way** once the rider picks it up, **Delivered** once he marks it delivered (these move to the top). When you open a delivered order, a green box shows what the rider recorded, for example *rider collected ₹290 cash · 3:10 pm*, and the payment is already filled in. Check the money, then press **Settle & save**.

## 4. Finding an old order or bill
Press **Find order or bill** and type the order number.
- **Reprint** the bill.
- **Resend to screen** for any kitchen ticket.
- Owners can **Void** a paid bill. A reason is required, and the order reopens for correction.

## 5. Orders page
Open **Orders** in the left menu to see every order, paid or not.
- **Dates**: pick From / To, or tap **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days** or **This month**. Dates are business days, so an order settled at 12:30 am is listed under the day before.
- **Filters**: Dine-in / Takeaway / Delivery, a status (Open, Billed, Paid, Cancelled), and search by order number, table (e.g. `G1`), customer name or phone.
- The top line shows how many orders there are, the total paid, and how many aren't paid yet.
- Tap an order to see its items (cancelled ones struck out), the bill, how it was paid and who settled it, the delivery status, and its kitchen tickets.
- **Print**:
  - **Print bill** prints a bill that isn't paid yet.
  - **Reprint bill** prints a copy of a paid one, marked REPRINT.
  - **Delivery bill** prints the rider's slip.
  - If an open order has no bill yet, **Generate & print bill** creates and prints it, just like on the billing desk.

## 6. Closing the counter (end of the day)
We work past midnight, so sales are saved by **business day**, not by the calendar. The date on the billing desk (for example **Fri, 27 Nov**) is the day the counter opened. A bill settled at 12:40 am still counts for Fri, 27 Nov.

When the last customer has paid:
1. Press **Close counter**. You see the day's bills, total sales, cash and UPI. If orders are still open, they're listed. Settle or cancel them first if you can.
2. Press **Close counter** (or **Close anyway** if orders are open). That day is now closed, and any new sale goes to the next day.

Pressed it by mistake? Press **Undo close** straight away, or open **Close counter** again and press **Reopen**. This works only until the first bill of the new day is settled.

Forgot to close? The day ends on its own at 6 am, so the next morning starts fresh.

## 7. Accounts
Open **Accounts** in the left menu to record vendor bills and payments and daily salary payments. See the accounts guide. You can delete only the entries you made today; ask a manager to fix older ones.

## 8. Alerts
- **Station screen offline**: tickets are saved and appear when the screen reconnects. Tell the kitchen if it's urgent and check the tablet's power/WiFi.
- **Tickets not shown on a kitchen screen**: press **Resend all**, or open **Details** to resend one at a time.
- **Bill print failed**: check the printer's paper and power, then press **Print again**.
