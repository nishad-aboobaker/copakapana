# Cashier Guide — Copakapana Restaurant

## 1. Signing in
Open `/login/pc` on the counter PC and sign in with your username and password. You land on the **Billing desk**. The menu on the left also has **Take orders**, if you need to enter an order yourself.

## 2. The billing desk
- **Top strip**: each kitchen screen and the bill printer shown green (online) or red (offline).
- **Left**: all open orders. Orders waiting for the bill are listed first and tagged **Bill asked** or **Billed**. Search by order number, table (e.g. `T5`) or customer name/phone, or filter by Dine-in / Takeaway / Delivery.
- **Middle**: the selected order's items and totals.
- **Right**: payment.

Selecting an order only *shows* it. Nothing changes until you press **Generate bill**.

## 3. Closing a bill
1. Select the order and press **Generate bill**. This locks the items and marks the table as billing.
2. Optional: **Add discount**. Choose percent or amount, pick or type a reason (required). The new total is previewed before you apply.
3. Take payment:
   - **Cash**: enter the cash received, or tap a quick amount (*Exact*, ₹500, …). The change to return is shown in green.
   - **UPI**: confirm the payment on your UPI app, then settle.
   - **Split**: enter the cash part; the rest is put on UPI automatically.
4. Press **Settle & print**. The table is freed, the receipt prints, and a card shows the bill number, the amount and any change, with a **Reprint** button.

**Delivery orders:** after **Generate bill**, press **Print delivery bill** and send it with the rider. It shows the customer's name, phone and address in large print, the items, and **COLLECT ₹…** (the amount to take from the customer). When the rider comes back with the money, settle the bill as usual. If the customer already paid by UPI, settle first, then press **Print delivery bill**: the slip says **PAID**, so the rider collects nothing.

## 4. Finding an old order or bill
Press **Find order or bill** and type the order number.
- **Reprint** the bill.
- **Resend to screen** for any kitchen ticket.
- Owners can **Void** a paid bill. A reason is required, and the order reopens for correction.

## 5. Alerts
- **Station screen offline**: tickets are saved and appear when the screen reconnects. Tell the kitchen if it's urgent and check the tablet's power/WiFi.
- **Tickets not shown on a kitchen screen**: press **Resend all**, or open **Details** to resend one at a time.
- **Bill print failed**: check the printer's paper and power, then press **Print again**.
