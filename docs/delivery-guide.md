# Delivery staff guide

## 1. Signing in
1. Open the app on your phone (the same link waiters use).
2. Tap your name. It has a small **Delivery** tag under it.
3. Enter your 4-digit PIN.

You go straight to **Deliveries**. Delivery accounts can only use this screen.

## 2. To deliver
Every delivery order appears here as soon as it is taken, oldest first. Each card shows:
- **#number** and **To pick up** (orange) or **On the way** (blue).
- **Cooking** / **Food ready**: whether the kitchen has finished the food.
- The customer's **name and address** in large text.
- **Phone button**: tap to call the customer. **Map**: opens the address in Google Maps.
- **Items**: tap to see what's in the order.
- **Collect ₹…**: the amount to take from the customer. **Paid · nothing to collect** means the customer already paid.

If the card says *Bill not made yet*, ask the cashier to generate the bill and give you the delivery bill.

## 3. Delivering
1. When you leave with the food, tap **Picked up · on the way**. The billing desk sees that it's on the way.
2. At the customer, tap **Delivered**. Choose **Cash** or **UPI**, check the amount, and confirm.
   If the order was already paid, just tap **Mark delivered**.
3. Tapped by mistake? Use **Not picked up yet** or **Undo** on the card.

Your phone buzzes when a new delivery comes in.

## 4. End of the day: Done today
The **Done today** tab lists everything you delivered and shows **Cash to hand over**. Give that cash to the cashier. The cashier sees exactly what you recorded for each order and settles the bills.
