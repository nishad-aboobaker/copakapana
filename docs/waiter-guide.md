# Waiter Guide — Copakapana Restaurant

## 1. Signing in
1. Open the app on your phone (or tap the installed **Copakapana** icon).
2. Tap your name, then enter your 4-digit PIN. It signs in as soon as the 4th digit is entered.
3. Your name is remembered and shown first next time.
> 5 wrong PINs lock your account for 5 minutes. The screen tells you how many tries are left.

## 2. The three tabs (bottom of the screen)
- **Tables**: the floor plan. Switch between **Ground** and **First** floor.
  - White = free · Amber = occupied · Red = waiting for the bill.
- **My orders**: every order you've opened that isn't paid yet, including takeaway and delivery. Tap one to reopen it.
- **Ready**: food the kitchen has marked ready. Your phone vibrates and the tab shows a green count. Tap **Served** once you've picked it up.

## 3. Taking an order
1. Tap a **free table** (or **Takeaway** / **Delivery** at the top).
2. In **Menu**, pick a category or search.
   - Tap **+** to add a dish instantly.
   - Tap the dish name to choose a size (Half / Full), quantity and a kitchen note. Quick notes like *Less spicy* are one tap.
3. The orange bar at the bottom shows how many items and the total. Tap **Review items**.
4. Adjust quantities, then tap **Send to kitchen**. Each kitchen station gets its ticket on its screen right away.
   - Dishes tagged **Counter** (soft drinks, water) are not sent to the kitchen — serve them from the counter. If the order has only counter items, the button says **Add to order**.

## 4. After sending
- **Sent** shows everything on the order with live kitchen status: *Sent → Preparing → Ready*.
- **Add more**: go back to **Menu**, add dishes and send again. The kitchen sees the new items added in green on the same card, with no new ticket. If the kitchen has already marked that card Ready, the new items go as a small **Extra** card.
- **Cancel an item**: tap **×** next to it and confirm. The kitchen sees it struck out on the card. Cancellations are logged.
- **Print bill**: tap **Print bill**. The bill prints on the counter printer; collect it and give it to the table. The table turns red and the billing desk sees the order as **Billed**. Need another copy? Tap **Print bill again**. Payment is still taken and settled at the billing desk.

## 5. Takeaway & delivery
1. Tap **Takeaway** or **Delivery** on the Tables tab.
2. Type the customer's 10-digit mobile number. Returning customers' names and saved addresses fill in automatically.
3. Tap **Continue to menu** and order as usual.

## 6. If the WiFi drops
- A red **Offline** badge appears at the top. Keep taking orders; they're saved on your phone (**Queued** badge).
- When the connection is back, they send automatically, exactly once.
- If the server refuses a queued order (e.g. the table was taken meanwhile), you'll see a message. Re-enter that order.
