# Accounts guide (vendors and staff salary)

Open **Accounts** in the left menu. Cashiers, managers and owners can use it.

## 1. Vendors
Shops and suppliers you buy from on credit (chicken, vegetables, drinks agency…).

**Add a vendor:** press **Add vendor** and enter the name and the **opening balance (OB)**: what you already owed them before you started using the app. Phone and note are optional.

**Every day:** select the vendor and fill **Add bill / payment**:
- **Today's bill**: the amount on their bill.
- **Paid to them**: what you paid (Cash, UPI or Bank).
- Fill one or both, then **Save**. The new balance is shown before you save.

The vendor page shows total bills, total paid, the **balance to pay**, and a **statement** with the balance after every entry. The list on the left shows each vendor's balance and the total owed to all vendors.

## 2. Staff salary
This list is separate from the app logins, so you can add anyone (cooks, cleaners, helpers).

**Add staff:** press **Add staff** and enter:
- **Monthly salary.**
- **Outstanding salary**: salary you still owed them before today. Use a minus amount if they already took an advance.
- **Salary starts from**: usually today.

**How salary is counted:** salary is earned **day by day**. Each day adds the monthly salary ÷ the number of days in that month. So on the 10th of a 30-day month, a ₹18,000 salary has earned ₹6,000 that month. A full month always adds exactly the monthly salary.

**Paying daily:** select the person, enter the amount under **Pay salary** (or tap ₹200 / ₹500 / ₹1000, or **Full** to clear the balance), and press **Save payment**.

The page shows:
- **Earned this month** and **Paid this month**.
- **Balance to pay** = outstanding + everything earned − everything paid. If you have paid more than they earned, it shows **Advance paid**.
- **Month by month**: earned, paid and the balance at the end of each month.
- **Payments**: every payment with its date.

**Salary change:** press **Edit** and change the monthly salary. Choose the date the new salary applies from. Earlier days keep the old salary.

**Someone leaves:** press **Edit** and turn off **Still working here**. Their salary stops after today. Pay what is left and their balance goes to zero. They stay in the reports.

## 3. Report
Pick the dates (or **Today**, **This month**, **Last month**). You get:
- Totals: vendor bills, paid to vendors, salary earned, salary paid, and the total paid out.
- **Vendors**: balance at the start, bills, paid, balance at the end, per vendor.
- **Staff salary**: monthly salary, balance at the start, earned, paid, balance at the end, per person.
- **Day by day**: vendor bills, payments to vendors, salary paid and total paid out each day.

Press **CSV** to download it for Excel, or **Print**.

## 4. Mistakes
Press the bin icon next to an entry or payment to delete it. The balances are recalculated.
- **Cashiers** can delete only the entries they made today.
- **Managers and owners** can delete any entry.

Every addition, change and deletion is recorded in the audit log.
